Common Problems

What Goes Wrong on a JennAir

Before you call anyone, notice exactly when the symptom happens. On built-in refrigeration the timing tells us more than the symptom itself, and it often changes the price of the repair.

🔊 Noise That Stops at the Door

Open the door. If the sound stops, it is the evaporator fan, because the door switch cuts power to it. If it carries on, we are looking at the condenser fan or the compressor. Thirty seconds of your attention here can be the difference between a few hundred dollars and a sealed system job.

🌑 Obsidian Interior Gone Dark

JennAir builds the dark interior around its lighting, so a failed LED strip or driver leaves you genuinely unable to see what is in there. Nothing about the cooling is affected, which is why it gets postponed, but it is normally a simple part and worth adding to any other visit.

🌡️ Temperature Will Not Settle

Too cold one day, too warm the next, with nobody touching the setting. That is a sensor and damper problem, not a cooling problem. A unit that cannot read itself accurately overshoots and then overcorrects, and the compressor pays for it in run hours.

🧊 Ice Maker Slowing or Stopped

Hollow cubes, half-full bins, then nothing. The assembly is independent of the refrigeration side, so it fails on its own schedule. Fill valve, module, or a fill tube frozen shut, and the stage it stopped at tells us which.

❄️ Frost in the Freezer Drawer

On bottom-freezer and column units, frost creeping across the drawer means the defrost circuit is not clearing as it should. Left alone the ice sheet blocks airflow, and the fresh food side starts warming for what looks like an unrelated reason.

🚪 Panel Door Out of Line

Custom panels add real weight, and the hinge eventually settles under it. First you notice the door sitting proud against the cabinetry, then condensation along the seal, then a temperature the unit cannot hold. Catching it at the alignment stage is the cheap version.

Models We Service

JennAir Refrigeration We Work On

The model number is on a plate inside the fresh food compartment, usually on the upper left wall. Read it to us on the phone and the visit gets shorter.

Refrigerator and Freezer Columns

Separate refrigerator and freezer towers, each with its own cooling system. They also fail separately, so one column being fine says nothing about the other.

Built-In French Door

Two doors above, freezer drawer below. Ice maker faults and drawer frost are the two reasons we are usually called to these.

Built-In Side-by-Side

The 42" and 48" units, panel-ready or stainless. Hinge settle and door alignment come up often here because of the panel weight.

RISE and NOIR Expressions

The two JennAir design families. The styling differs, the refrigeration underneath does not, so parts and diagnosis are the same either way.

Undercounter and Beverage

Compact JennAir refrigeration. Temperature drift and fan noise are the usual complaints, and both are normally straightforward.
