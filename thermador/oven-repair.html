Common Problems

What Actually Goes Wrong on a Thermador Oven

Every brand fails in its own way. These are the six we see on Thermador in DFW homes, and what each one really means before anyone quotes you a part.

🔥 Won't Heat, or Takes Forever

On gas Pro Grand and Pro Harmony ranges the glow-bar igniter weakens with age. It still lights up orange, so it looks fine, but it no longer draws enough current to open the gas safety valve. If your oven now needs 20 minutes to reach 350°F, this is almost always why.

🌡️ Baking Off Temperature

Cakes sinking, edges burning, roasts underdone. The RTD temperature sensor drifts as it ages and starts reporting a false reading to the control. We measure its actual resistance cold rather than guessing, then replace or recalibrate.

🔵 Blue Knob Lights Dead

The illuminated blue knob ring is a Thermador signature, and when a segment goes dark the range looks broken even though it cooks fine. Usually the LED module or its harness connection, not the control board. Worth knowing before someone sells you a board.

🚪 Door Sagging or Not Sealing

Professional doors are heavy, and after years of use the hinges lose tension. The door sits slightly open at the top, heat escapes, and the oven never holds temperature. Hinges are replaceable — the door and gasket usually are not the problem.

🔲 Error Code on the Display

Common on Professional and Masterpiece wall ovens, often the relay board rather than the main control. Tell us the exact code when you call and we will bring the likely part with us instead of ordering it after the visit.

💨 Steam Oven Not Filling

On Masterpiece steam and combination ovens: reservoir not drawing, error after filling, or weak steam. Scale buildup from hard North Texas water is the usual cause, and descaling is often the entire repair.
