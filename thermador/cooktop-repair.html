Common Problems

Thermador Cooktop Problems We Fix

Star Burner ports blocked

Thermador's star-shaped burner ports are the brand's signature and they clean differently from a round port ring — the points trap boil-over that a quick wipe misses. The result is a flame that lights on one side and creeps round, or will not carry at all.

Continuous clicking after a spill

Moisture in the spark switch keeps the igniter firing even once a burner is lit. Common after a boil-over on sealed-burner Thermador tops, and it is a safety item rather than a nuisance — the spark module should not be left firing.

ExtraLow simmer not holding

Thermador's low-simmer settings cycle the burner rather than running it continuously. When the cycling control or the valve drifts, the symptom is scorching on a setting that used to hold chocolate or sauce, not a dead burner.

Induction zone dead, others fine

On Thermador induction a single dead zone usually points to that zone’s coil or its flex connector, not the whole board. Each zone gets tested independently before anything is quoted — boards on induction tops are among the pricier parts on the appliance.

Pan not recognised on induction

Induction needs ferrous cookware and a minimum base diameter. Before condemning electronics we confirm the pan actually couples — a surprising share of "induction not working" calls are cookware, not the cooktop.

Touch controls unresponsive

Control panel or board failure on electric and induction tops. Heat soak from the oven below, or moisture under the glass, produces the same symptom — which is why the panel gets tested hot as well as cold.
