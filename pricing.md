### Commercial Refrigeration — Walk-In Coolers & Freezers
- Price: $90 diagnostic, flat-rate quote after on-site assessment — no fixed range published, because the number is driven by the fault, the age of the condensing unit, and the refrigerant it runs
- Scope: evaporator icing and defrost-cycle faults, condenser fouling, refrigerant leak location and recovery, compressor replacement, contactors and relays, thermostats and temperature probes, condensate drain lines, door gaskets, hinges and closers, panel damage
- Sealed-system work performed in-house (compressor, refrigerant, evaporator, condenser) — not subcontracted
- Refrigerants: legacy R-22 and current R-448A / R-449A / R-290. R-22 scarcity is the main driver of repair-versus-replace on older condensing units
- Emergency and after-hours response available for commercial accounts with product at risk
- Written service record provided per visit — usable for health-department follow-up and spoilage/insurance claims
- Preventive maintenance contracts: from $349/year per location (coil cleaning, gasket and door inspection, temperature-log verification, drain-line service)
- Page: https://acdcdfw.com/walk-in-cooler-repair.html
