Common Problems

The KitchenAid Faults We See Most

KitchenAid builds a different kind of oven than the pro-style ranges, and it fails in different places. Here is what actually brings us out, and what each symptom usually means.

💧 Aqualift Leaves It Dirty

The most common KitchenAid complaint we hear, and often nothing is broken. Aqualift is a low-temperature steam clean, not a high-heat burn-off, so baked-on grease survives it by design. If yours also fails to drain or throws a fault, that is a real repair and we can tell the difference.

🌀 Even-Heat Convection Not Even

Cookies done at the front, pale at the back. The convection element that rings the fan can fail while the regular bake element carries on, so the oven still heats and nothing looks obviously wrong. It is one part, and it is usually the whole fix.

🔲 Panel Dead or Unresponsive

Buttons that need pressing twice, a display that goes blank, or an oven that will not start at all. On built-in wall ovens this is the control board or the touch panel above it, and those are two different parts at two different prices. We test which before ordering either.

🍗 Meat Probe Reading Wrong

The temperature probe drifts with age and starts calling a roast done early. If you have stopped trusting it you are probably right. The probe and its jack are serviceable separately from the oven control.

🛤️ Glide Racks Sticking

The roll-out extension racks bind or drop on one side once the bearings collect grease and the rails distort from heat. Small annoyance, quick fix, and worth doing while we are already there for something else.

🚪 Door Not Closing Square

A gap at one corner means the hinge has weakened or the inner glass has shifted in its frame. Heat escapes, the oven runs cool against the dial, and every recipe drifts. Hinges are replaceable without touching the rest of the door.

Models We Service

KitchenAid Ovens We Work On

The model number is on the frame behind the door or along the bottom edge. It starts with a K and it saves everyone a trip.

Double Wall Ovens

Model numbers usually beginning KODE or KODC. Two cavities, two sets of elements, and often only one of them is actually faulty.

Single Wall Ovens

The KOSE and KOSC family. The most common KitchenAid built-in we see in DFW kitchens.

Combination Microwave-Ovens

KOCE units with the microwave stacked above the oven. Two appliances in one cabinet, so the diagnosis has to separate them.

Slide-In and Freestanding Ranges

Gas, electric and dual-fuel, including the induction slide-ins. Oven cavity, convection and control work.
