What This Is

Written to Save You a Service Call, Not to Sell You One

Most appliance-repair blogs exist to rank for a keyword and then dump you into a phone number. This one is written differently, because we run the calls ourselves: seven years on high-end built-in appliances and commercial refrigeration in DFW, and a fairly clear view of how many service calls did not need to happen.

So each guide does the same four things. It names the symptom precisely — “not cooling” and “not making ice” each cover four different faults, and which one you have narrows the cause immediately. It lists what you can safely check yourself, and just as importantly what you should not touch. It is straight about how the repair gets quoted — on site, flat-rate, before any work starts — rather than making you call to find that out. And it is honest about when a repair stops making sense — on a built-in the answer is almost always repair; on a scaled ice machine head with a discontinued board it sometimes is not.

The equipment we write about

Three lines, because that is what we actually work on:

Commercial refrigeration. Walk-in coolers and freezers, reach-ins, prep and sandwich tables, back-bar and undercounter units. This is a different trade from appliance repair, and most of what goes wrong — fouled condensers, stuck defrost cycles, drifted probes, refrigerant leaks — is invisible until product temperature is already out of range. Service pages: walk-in cooler repair and commercial equipment repair.

Commercial ice machines. Scotsman, Manitowoc and Hoshizaki. In North Texas the story is nearly always water — hard supply, scale on the evaporator, and a filter nobody changed. Service page: ice machine repair.

High-end built-in appliances. Sub-Zero, Wolf, Viking, Thermador and the rest of the built-in line in Southlake, Highland Park, Westlake and Colleyville kitchens — refrigeration, ovens, ranges and wine cabinets. These are appliances where the failure is one part, not the whole machine, and where replacement usually means cabinetry work. Service pages: refrigerator repair and range & oven repair.

One standing note on price: our on-site diagnostic is credited back in full when you approve the repair, and you get a flat-rate quote before any part is ordered. Where a guide gives a cost range it is a real range from real jobs — but no one, including us, can quote a specific fault by phone.
